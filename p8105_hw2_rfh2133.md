The baseline file required skipping the two header rows and assigning
useful variable names. I recoded sex and APOE4 carrier status as factors
and removed participants who had MCI at baseline. This left 479 eligible
participants; 93 developed MCI during follow-up. The average baseline
age was 65 years, and 30% of women were APOE4 carriers.

The amyloid data were reshaped from wide to long format so that each row
represents one participant at one study visit. Visit times were coded as
0, 2, 4, 6, and 8 years from baseline, and missing biomarker
measurements were retained as missing values.

There are 8 participants who appear only in the cleaned baseline data
(IDs 14, 49, 92, 179, 268, 304, 389, 412) and 16 who appear only in the
amyloid data (IDs 72, 234, 283, 380, 484, 485, 486, 487, 488, 489, 490,
491, 492, 493, 494, 495). This shows that the two datasets do not
contain exactly the same participants. After keeping only participants
found in both datasets, the combined dataset contains 471 participants
and 2355 participant-visit observations. It includes baseline
demographics, APOE4 carrier status, MCI onset information, visit time,
and amyloid beta 42/40 ratio. The combined dataset was exported as
`mci_complete.csv`.
